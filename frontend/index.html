Crop Health Report

🍅 Tomato

Early Blight

Alternaria solani

Severity Moderate
87% AI Match

🌿 Symptoms

  • Brown spots on leaves with concentric ring markings
  • Yellowing around the affected leaf areas
  • Lower leaves turning dark and dropping prematurely

🛡 Prevention

  • Improve air circulation around plants
  • Avoid overhead watering; water at soil level
  • Rotate crops regularly

🌱 Treatment Options

🌿 Organic
  • Copper-based organic spray
  • Neem oil solution (5ml/L)
  • Prune infected leaves
🧪 Chemical
  • Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2g/L
  • Chlorothalonil spray
  • Repeat after 12 days
⚠
Safety Information

Follow product label instructions. Wear protective equipment (mask, gloves) when applying sprays.
